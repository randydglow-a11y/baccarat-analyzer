How to use

Tap the winner after each hand. Undo corrects the most recent entry. Your session is saved locally on this device, and you can export it as CSV.

Baccarat outcomes are uncertain. Past results, patterns and streaks cannot reliably predict the next hand. This tool does not recommend bets.
