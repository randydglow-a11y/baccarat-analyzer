Reference strategies discussed for live play and future testing. These are pattern-based rules, not proven profitable systems. No strategy changes baccarat's house edge.

1. Two-Row Main Road — Match/Mismatch Streak

New • Interactive signal guide

Lay non-tie outcomes into columns of two, top to bottom. Compare the current position to the same row in the previous column. Match = same outcome; mismatch = opposite.

After three consecutive matches or mismatches, choose whether to predict continuation or reversal. One flat wager on the next eligible outcome; ties push and do not change the pattern. After a win or loss, wait for a fresh three-result trigger.

2. Cockroach Road — Match/Mismatch

Compare the derived road indicator with the corresponding prior-column mark. Select Banker or Player only when exactly one prospective result produces the chosen match or mismatch condition. Configurable row count, one-hit-per-column or shoe rules, and random shoe entry.

3. Column-Hit Selection

Wait for a qualifying road signal, then limit betting to a specified number of resolved attempts in that column. Stop betting the column after its first win or after the attempt limit.

4. Hybrid Recovery Progression

Discussed progression: 1, 3, 7, 15, 15, 30, 30 units, with optional two-loss step advancement, step-down after a recovery win, and carryover across shoes. Escalating stakes can sharply increase drawdown.

5. Column-Loss Exposure Recovery

Track the net loss of an entire column and use the accumulated deficit to inform the next column's progression. This is a proposed concept, not an established profitable method.

6. Flat Banker Baseline

Flat stake on Banker only, with a fixed session loss limit. Banker typically pays 0.95:1 and retains a house edge.

7. 3-Row Follow + Opposite Recovery

Two-column cycle • $5 / $15 / $30 / $60 / $120

Normal column: Ignore ties when counting rows. Observe Row 1 without betting. On Row 2 bet $5 on the same side as Row 1. If that loses, repeat the selection on Row 3 for $15. Stop betting the column after the first win.

Recovery column: Only if both normal bets lose, start betting immediately on Row 1 of the next column, choosing the opposite of the previous column's Row 1. Keep that side for up to three resolved bets: $30, then $60, then $120, stopping on the first win.

Reset: After the recovery column, reset both selection and progression for the following column, whether the recovery wins or loses. If the normal column wins, the following column also starts a fresh normal cycle. Ties push and repeat the pending bet without advancing the row or stake.

Maximum full-cycle exposure: $230, with up to five losing bets. Player wins after the first stake yield +$5, +$10, +$10, +$10, or +$10 net across the cycle; Banker payouts are lower due to commission. A complete losing cycle is -$230. This strategy does not eliminate the house edge.
