Strategy definitions and limits

#1 Two-row streak: Rows fixed at two; compare same-row outcomes across adjacent columns, wait for consecutive matches or mismatches, then bet continuation/reversal of that relation once.

#2 Cockroach road: Uses the Analyzer's Big Road derived-road implementation to project Banker/Player signals. Bets only when exactly one choice meets the selected continuation/reversal rule. If the road engine cannot load, this strategy is marked unavailable rather than replaced with a different rule.

#3 Column-hit: Uses the same derived-road signal as #2, stops after the first winning bet or configured attempt limit in each column.

#4 Hybrid progression: Uses the same derived-road signal and configurable unit ladder, stepping up after two losses and down after a win; resets to the first step when cumulative session profit turns positive. This is an illustrative implementation of the hybrid concept.

#5 Column-loss exposure: Uses the same derived-road signal; stakes target the previous column's unrecovered loss plus one base unit, subject to the stake cap. This is an illustrative implementation, not a previously finalized rule.

#6 Flat Banker: Always bets Banker at the base stake.

#7 Three-row follow + opposite recovery: Fixed three rows per column. Observe normal Row 1, then bet the first two amounts from the editable Strategy #7 stakes on Rows 2/3; if both lose, bet opposite of that first Row 1 on all three rows of the next column using the remaining three amounts. Reset after recovery. No extra bets are made after a column's first win.

#2–#5 are configurable experimental implementations. Review their assumptions before interpreting a result as a test of your exact live-play rules. The maximum stake setting skips bets above the cap; it does not force smaller recovery bets.
